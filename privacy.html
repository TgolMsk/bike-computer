一句话总结：我们不收集你的任何数据。本 App 没有服务器、没有账号、没有广告，也不包含任何统计或第三方 SDK。 你的位置和骑行记录只保存在你自己的 Apple Watch、iPhone 和“健康”App 里。

本 App 会用到哪些数据

位置

在 Apple Watch 上用 GPS 计算骑行速度、里程和海拔，以及在导航时判断你走到了路线的哪里。位置只在你的设备上处理，不会发送给我们。

导航搜索和路线规划

搜索目的地、规划骑行路线和生成路径图上的地图，由 Apple 地图（MapKit）提供。为此，你输入的地名、当前位置附近的区域和起终点会发送给 Apple， 适用 Apple 的隐私政策。我们看不到这些内容。

健康

经你授权后，每次骑行会作为一条“体能训练”（包括用时、骑行距离和路线）写入“健康”App；App 还会读取骑行距离，用于在 App 意外退出后接上中断的骑行。 健康数据只保存在你的设备上，不会发送给我们，不用于广告，也不会分享给任何第三方。你可以随时在“健康”App 里查看、删除这些数据或关闭授权。

骑行记录

每次骑完，完整记录（轨迹、速度、时间、目的地名称）先存在手表上，再通过 Apple 的手表与手机传输功能发送到与手表配对的你自己的 iPhone， 用来生成路径图。记录只在这两台设备之间传输，不经过我们的任何服务器。

照片

只有当你在 iPhone 上点“保存到相册”时，App 才会把路径图写入你的相册；App 不会读取你的照片。

设置

皮肤、速度数字样式、常用地点（家、公司）和最近的目的地等设置保存在设备本地。

我们不做的事

  • 不收集、不存储、不出售任何个人信息
  • 不追踪你，不使用广告标识符，没有广告
  • 不包含任何第三方统计、崩溃收集或广告 SDK
  • 不要求注册或登录

删除数据

  • iPhone 上的骑行记录：在记录列表里左滑删除
  • “健康”里的体能训练和路线：在“健康”App 里删除
  • 删除 App 会一并删除它在设备上保存的全部数据

儿童

本 App 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不收集任何人的个人信息。

政策变更

如果本政策有变化，我们会更新本页面并修改上面的生效日期。

Bike Computer (Watch) — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026

In short: we do not collect any data. The app has no server, no account, no ads, and no analytics or third‑party SDKs. Your location and ride records stay on your own Apple Watch, iPhone and in the Health app.

Data the app uses

Location

Used on Apple Watch to measure speed, distance and altitude, and to follow your progress along a navigation route. It is processed on your device and never sent to us.

Search and routing

Place search, cycling directions and the map in route images are provided by Apple Maps (MapKit). The search text, the area around you and the route endpoints are sent to Apple and handled under Apple's Privacy Policy. We never see them.

Health

With your permission, each ride is saved to the Health app as a workout (duration, cycling distance and route). The app also reads cycling distance to resume a ride after an unexpected exit. Health data stays on your devices, is never sent to us, never used for advertising and never shared with third parties. You can review, delete or revoke access in the Health app at any time.

Ride records

After each ride, the full record (track, speeds, times, destination name) is stored on the watch and transferred to your own paired iPhone using Apple's Watch Connectivity, where it is used to draw the route image. It never passes through any server of ours.

Photos

The app writes a route image to your photo library only when you tap “Save”. It never reads your photos.

Settings

Skins, digit style, saved places (Home, Work) and recent destinations are stored locally on the device.

What we don't do

  • No collection, storage or sale of personal information
  • No tracking, no advertising identifier, no ads
  • No third‑party analytics, crash reporting or ad SDKs
  • No sign‑up or login

Deleting data

  • Ride records on iPhone: swipe left in the ride list to delete
  • Workouts and routes: delete them in the Health app
  • Deleting the app removes all data it stored on the device

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
